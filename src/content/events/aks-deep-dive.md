## Overview

A practical deep dive into **Azure Kubernetes Service** for teams moving .NET
containers to production — beyond `hello world`, into the operational concerns
that actually decide whether a cluster survives real traffic.

## What we covered

- **Cluster setup** — node pools, sizing, and the managed control plane.
- **Deployments** — manifests, health probes, and rolling updates.
- **Scaling** — the Horizontal Pod Autoscaler and the cluster autoscaler under load.
- **Networking** — ingress, TLS, and exposing services safely.
- **Secrets & config** — Azure Key Vault integration and workload identity.
- **Observability** — Azure Monitor, Container Insights, and reading the signals that matter.

## Who attended

Backend and platform engineers operating containerised services on Azure. 110+ attended.
