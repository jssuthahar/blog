AI has changed what work in IT looks like faster than most courses or job descriptions have caught up. This series answers the questions students and IT professionals ask most: Should I still learn to code? Is my degree still worth it? What should I learn, and in what order? Which jobs are growing? And if I already work in IT, what do I learn now? Short, honest parts, each answering one question.
