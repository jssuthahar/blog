One AI coding assistant writes code. A software project needs about thirty jobs done, and only a few of them are typing. This series follows one Flutter and Firebase app from kickoff to production, and builds the agents, skills, prompts and hooks that covered every other job: planning, architecture, testing, security, licenses, accessibility, release and the rest.
