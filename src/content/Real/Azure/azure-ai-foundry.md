# From a model to a shipped AI product

Topic: What Azure AI Foundry is, why businesses adopt it, and what teams build with it
Runtime: ~22s across 8 stages (1080x1920)

## Caption

Most AI projects do not fail at the model. They fail at everything after it.

Azure AI Foundry is Microsoft’s answer to that gap: a model catalog you can swap without rewriting your app, grounding on your own data through Azure AI Search, the Foundry Agent Service for tools and actions, evaluations and content filters before release, and Entra ID, RBAC, private networking and tracing once it is live.

Same platform behind a support copilot, a document pipeline, a retail assistant or an engineering agent.

Which of these are you building first?

## Stage breakdown

01. **The demo is easy. Shipping is not** (2600ms) — Models, your data, evaluation, safety and monitoring — five separate tools, glued together by hand.
02. **Azure AI Foundry is one platform** (2700ms) — The catalog, the grounding, the agents, the evaluations and the guardrails live in a single project.
03. **Pick a model, not a lock-in** (2800ms) — Frontier and open models sit behind one endpoint shape, so changing model is a config change, not a rewrite.
04. **Ground it in your own data** (2900ms) — Azure AI Search indexes your content and retrieves the right passages, so the answer ships with citations.
05. **Agents that actually do the work** (2900ms) — Foundry Agent Service gives the model instructions, tools and a thread — so it can act on your systems, not just chat.
06. **Evaluate before your users do** (2800ms) — Score groundedness, relevance and risk on every change, with content filters and red teaming ahead of release.
07. **Enterprise controls come built in** (2600ms) — It deploys inside your own identity, network and compliance boundary, with tracing on every request.
08. **This is what teams ship with it** (2700ms) — Support copilots, document processing, retail assistants, engineering agents — one platform underneath all of them.
