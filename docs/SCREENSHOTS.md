# Screenshot backlog

Published articles carrying no image. A screenshot of your own screen is the
one artifact on a page that nobody else could have produced. It does more to
show the work is yours than any amount of prose editing.

`scripts/check-humanizer.mjs` flags every published post with zero images, so
this list regenerates itself: fix a post, and it drops off the warning list.

## How to fill a slot

Each article below has `{/* SCREENSHOT: … */}` markers at the spots where a
capture would land best. Take the shot, drop the PNG in the folder's
`images/` directory, then replace the whole marker line with the image line it
already spells out for you.

Two rules worth keeping: capture your real screen with real project names and
real output rather than a staged example, and crop to the part that matters.
