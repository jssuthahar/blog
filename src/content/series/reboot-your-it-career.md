AI has changed what an entry-level IT job asks of you, and most degree programmes have not caught up yet. This series is for students in computer science, software engineering, IT, cybersecurity, data, networking and cloud who want a plan rather than a panic: which fundamentals matter more now, how to use AI without hollowing out your own learning, what to build, and how to show an employer you can do the work.
