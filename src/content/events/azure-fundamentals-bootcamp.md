## Overview

A hands-on, single-day run through everything on the **AZ-900: Microsoft Azure
Fundamentals** exam — the fastest way to get grounded in the cloud before going
deeper into any Azure specialism.

## What we covered

- **Core concepts** — regions, availability zones, and the shared responsibility model.
- **Core services** — compute, storage, networking, and databases, with live portal demos.
- **Pricing & cost** — the pricing calculator, cost management, and how to avoid bill shock.
- **Governance & compliance** — RBAC, Azure Policy, and resource locks.
- **Exam prep** — question styles, timing, and a practice round.

## Who attended

Students and developers new to Azure, plus experienced engineers wanting a clean
AZ-900 pass. 180+ attendees joined live.
